Field Guide

Enemies: combat drones, recon speeders, heavy walkers, shield troopers. Armor reduces each hit — poison ignores armor.
🪨 Rocks and 🏚️ buildings block line of sight and can't be built on — but 💣 Cannon and 🚀 Missile lob over them. 🎯 Snipers hit anywhere on the map with a clear sight-line.
Tank bosses roll in every 10th wave: huge HP, regeneration, and they cost 10 lives if they leak.
Clear every wave to win — 30 on Easy, 40 on Medium, 50 on Hard. Each cleared round pays a bonus that grows with the wave number. Every tower has 3 upgrade paths (3 tiers each) but you can invest in at most 2 paths per tower — choose wisely! Sell refunds 70%. Space starts the next wave.
